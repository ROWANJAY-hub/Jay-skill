# Matcha product constraints

Read this file only when the current product is a matcha bowl, whisk, whisk holder, scoop, sieve, accessory, or set.

## Identity and counting

- Distinguish a whisk holder from a tool cup, storage tube, or package.
- A whisk nested inside a clear tube can still be a separate sales item when current evidence confirms both pieces.
- A bowl, cup, tray, cloth, case, tube, and sieve may be sold products, packaging, or props. Resolve each through the current BOM rather than habit.
- Treat spout shape, bowl height-to-width ratio, foot, glaze distribution, whisk-tine structure, sieve frame, and decoration count as identity features.
- For a confirmed bowl-and-whisk-holder two-piece set, the bowl and holder are the only sold pieces. Do not add a whisk, scoop, sieve, tube, tray, or package to hero, detail, or scene images unless the user confirms it as sold content.
- A newly uploaded matcha set replaces the previous set's color, glaze, piece count, packaging, and accessory structure. Rebuild P0 instead of carrying forward an earlier set.
- Preserve a pure solid color as solid, and preserve a directional gradient or kiln effect in its proved direction. Do not generalize a fine speckled or cloudy glaze into cracks, maze lines, or an unrelated pattern.

## Correct use

- During whisking, hold only the bamboo handle; the tines contact matcha liquid inside the bowl.
- Place a whisk tines-down over a holder only when a real whisk holder exists; keep the handle upward.
- Once a whisk is removed from a clear storage tube for use, show the tube empty or omit it. Do not create a second whisk.
- Place a sieve independently or above the bowl while sifting. Never balance it on a whisk holder or tool cup.
- Sift powder into the bowl, not onto its outer wall or the table.
- Pour only from a real spout. Keep the stream continuous and directed into the receiving cup; avoid liquid coating the outer bowl.
- For a specially shaped bowl, follow the source-proved or user-confirmed outlet and orientation even when it differs from a conventional pouring bowl. Do not mirror it.
- Allow only matcha powder, water, matcha liquid, or foam inside the mixing bowl. Ice belongs only in an explicitly finished drinking glass.
- If the first pouring attempt distorts a short spout, switch to whisking or a finished-drink scene instead of repeating the same risky action.

## Scene control

- Keep the action image focused on one operation; do not force a high-piece-count set into the frame.
- Separate non-sold cups, trays, towels, ingredients, or decorative tools from the confirmed set through position, depth, and visual weight.
- Preserve at least two SKU-specific visible features in an action image.
- In a bowl-and-holder two-piece scene, environmental cups, towels, plants, or drinks may appear only as clearly separated props. Do not stage extra matcha tools as if included in the sale.
