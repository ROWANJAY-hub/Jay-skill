# Live Market Research

Run this workflow for every genuinely new product image, original title, or title rewrite. Additional views of the same unchanged SKU may reuse same-day research only when product identity, configuration, packaging, and category route are unchanged.

## Build queries from the image

Establish verified product facts first. Build targeted queries from:

`[material or finish] + [exact product noun] + [distinctive structure] + [configuration or packaging] + [custom or wholesale intent]`

The query must match the pictured SKU. For example, a single bamboo whisk in a clear tube needs whisk-led research around verified prong count, tube packaging, and customization; it must not inherit `Matcha Set` language merely because set keywords are broader. A new bowl glaze, spout, motif, case, or accessory combination requires its own relevant queries.

## Minimum live check

1. Check current Alibaba listings or category pages first for B2B product nouns, customization language, category placement, packaging, and visible popularity or reorder signals.
2. Cross-check at least one relevant marketplace such as Amazon, Etsy, Faire, or another credible channel; use two when materially different consumer and wholesale wording needs reconciliation.
3. Open the pages that materially affect the title. Do not base a decisive claim on an unopened search snippet.
4. Record the access date, platform, query, closeness of the product match, useful phrase or signal, and limitation. Prefer pages showing the same product type and configuration.

## Evidence boundary

- User-provided Alibaba keyword and product-performance exports supply quantitative traffic and funnel evidence.
- Live marketplace research supplies current wording, configuration, positioning, and observable page signals; it does not create comparable search-volume or conversion metrics.
- Bestseller labels, ranks, reviews, reorder counts, prices, and result counts are platform-specific snapshots. State their source and date and never generalize them across platforms.
- Live consensus may break a wording tie or fill a gap, but it must not silently override newer, directly comparable internal performance data.
- Do not copy competitor titles. Extract only natural buyer language and relevant category structure.
- Do not inherit `Japanese`, `Made in Japan`, `Handmade`, `Organic`, `Food Grade`, certifications, dimensions, prong counts, components, or other claims unless verified for the current SKU.

## Decision and stopping rule

Use the findings to confirm or revise the protected primary phrase, secondary entrances, verified differentiators, and buyer-use wording. The final decision line must name the live-research date and platforms and cite the pages that materially influenced the title when links are available.

Append each materially useful, sufficiently matched page as a raw observation in `assets/learning-memory.json`. Preserve its query, platform, date, URL, product-match level, useful phrase or signal, and limitation. Synthesize a recurring pattern only under [continuous-learning.md](continuous-learning.md); never promote a single observation directly into a permanent rule or numeric traffic claim.

If live access fails or no sufficiently matching product can be found, disclose the gap and stop before presenting the title as research-backed final. Ask whether the user wants a provisional title based only on verified product facts and dated internal evidence.
