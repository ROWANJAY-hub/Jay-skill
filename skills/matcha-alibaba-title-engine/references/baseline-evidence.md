## Baseline selection guidance

- For broad sets, normally protect `Matcha Set` or `Matcha Kit` before adding attributes.
- For bowl-led SKUs, protect `Matcha Bowl`; use `Chawan` or `with Spout` when accurate and space permits.
- For portable leather/bag configurations, consider `Matcha Travel Set`, `Leather Case`, `Carry Bag`, or `Storage Bag` according to the actual structure.
- Preserve `Matcha Whisk` or `Matcha Whisk Set` only when the whisk is visible/included and the phrase reads naturally.
- Use color, motif, glaze, and packaging phrases as differentiators after the core entrance is secured.
- In this snapshot, `matcha set` (index 33) materially exceeds `matcha travel set` (index 2). For a complete leather-case set, normally protect `Matcha Set` and express portability through `Leather Travel Case` unless newer comparable evidence reverses that order.
- The product-performance sample is too small and too multi-keyword to allocate causal credit to individual words. Use category priors and smoothed rates until title-version comparisons accumulate.
